# Intermission: Vowels

*Intermissions are useful, but if they stress you out, you shouldn't read them.*

Japanese only has five vowels.
The five vowel kana are あ, い, う, え, and お.

<pre>
前に出て「あいうえお」を書いてみてください
Come to the front and try writing a-i-u-e-o.
</pre>

The quality of the vowels sometimes changes slightly depending on the sounds near them,
but not enough to make new vowels.

Unlike English,
Japanese has a very regular orthography,
meaning writing maps to sounds pretty consistently.
For our purposes,
the Japanese vowels are basically always pronounced the same way,
except when they are not pronounced at all.
More on that later.

Resources on Japanese vowel sounds are easy to find online.
Explaining sounds through text is a fool's errand.
